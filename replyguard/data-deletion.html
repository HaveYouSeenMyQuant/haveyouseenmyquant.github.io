Deleting your data

Replyguard · last updated 29 August 2026

This page explains how to have everything we hold about you erased. It applies whether you connected the app to your own account, or you simply left a comment on an account that uses it.

If you connected the app to your account

Open Instagram and go to Settings → Website Permissions → Apps and Websites, then remove Replyguard. Meta notifies us automatically and we erase your account ID, your access token, your settings and your reply history immediately. Nothing further is posted.

If you left a comment on an account that uses Replyguard

We may hold your Instagram user ID and the ID of your comment, for up to 90 days, so that we do not reply to the same comment twice. To have that erased sooner, email haveyouseenmyquant@gmail.com with the username you commented under. We will confirm within 30 days.

You do not need an account with us, and we will not ask you for anything beyond enough information to find the records.

What gets deleted

  • Your Instagram account or user ID
  • Any access token issued for your account
  • Your configured settings
  • Every record of a comment we handled and the reply we sent

These rows are removed from the database, not anonymised or archived. Once a deletion completes we hold no copy.

Checking a deletion

Every deletion request is given a confirmation code. If Meta initiated it on your behalf you can open https://<our-host>/deletion/<code> to see its status. If you emailed us, the reply carries the code.

What we cannot delete

Replies already posted publicly on Instagram belong to the account that posted them and live on Instagram, not with us. Ask that account's owner to remove a reply, or report it to Instagram.
