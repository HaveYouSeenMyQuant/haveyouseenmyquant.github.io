Privacy Policy

Replyguard · last updated 29 August 2026

Replyguard sends a direct message containing a link to people who comment a keyword on your Instagram posts. It works on Instagram Business and Creator accounts, on behalf of the person who owns the account. This policy describes exactly what it stores, why, and how to have it erased.

Who we are

Replyguard is operated by the owner of the Instagram account @have_you_seen_my_quant. Contact for any privacy question or request: haveyouseenmyquant@gmail.com.

What we store, and why

DataWhy we hold itHow long
Your Instagram account ID and username To know whose account to reply on Until you disconnect
An access token issued by Meta There is no way to post a reply without one Until you disconnect; refreshed while valid
Your keywords and the links you pair with them This is the rule set the service applies Until you delete them or disconnect
The ID of each comment we have already handled, and the message we sent So that a repeated delivery from Meta cannot make us message the same person twice. Instagram allows one private reply per comment and we enforce it on our side too. 90 days
The commenter's Instagram user ID So we can find and erase their records if they ask 90 days

What we do not store

  • We do not store your Instagram password. We never see it. Connection is by Meta's own login screen and we receive only a token.
  • We do not store commenters' profiles, follower lists, email addresses, or any contact details.
  • We do not store anything about people who have not commented on your posts.
  • We do not retain comment text beyond the 90-day window above.

What we never do with it

We do not sell, rent, or share your data or your commenters' data with anyone. We do not use it to build advertising profiles. We do not use it to train machine-learning models.

We do not send comment text to any third party. Keyword matching happens on our own server and the message we send is the text you configured.

Deleting your data

Three routes, all of which work:

  • Disconnect the app in Instagram under Settings → Apps and Websites. Meta notifies us and we erase your records.
  • Email us at haveyouseenmyquant@gmail.com. We respond within 30 days.
  • Follow the instructions on our data deletion page, which also explains how a commenter — not just an account owner — can have their records erased.

Deletion means deletion. Rows are removed, not anonymised.

Your rights

If you are in the UK or the EEA you have the right to access, correct, export or erase your personal data, and to object to its processing. Write to the address above and we will act within 30 days. You may also complain to your local supervisory authority; in the UK that is the Information Commissioner's Office.

Security

Tokens are held encrypted at rest and are never written to logs, error messages, or analytics. Access to the production system is limited to the operator named above.

Changes

If this policy changes materially we will notify connected accounts by email before the change takes effect.
