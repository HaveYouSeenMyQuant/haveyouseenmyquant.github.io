5 pirates, 100 coins — the boss keeps 98

game_theoryposted 28 Jul 2026

Five pirates have to split 100 gold coins, and the ruthless one at the top walks away with almost all of them

98 of the 100. And the vote still passes 3–2.

The rules: the most senior pirate proposes a split, everyone votes, a tie passes. If it's rejected he's out with nothing and the next in line proposes. All five are greedy and perfectly rational, and nobody votes yes unless the offer strictly beats what the next round would pay them.

Work backwards. 2 left → 100 · 0 (he votes for himself, a tie passes) 3 left → 99 · 0 · 1 (one coin buys the pirate who'd otherwise get nothing) 4 left → 99 · 0 · 1 · 0 5 left → 98 · 0 · 1 · 0 · 1

He needs 3 of 5 votes, so he buys the two cheapest ones for a single coin each. Offer 99 instead and every version of it dies 2–3.

Most people are certain he has to share more.
