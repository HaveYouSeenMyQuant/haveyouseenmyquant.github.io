Two ropes, one lighter: measure exactly 45 minutes

puzzlesposted 30 Jul 2026

Two ropes. Each burns end to end in exactly one hour — but unevenly. One lighter. Measure exactly 45 minutes.

Read the rules carefully, because the obvious answer is wrong.

Each rope takes exactly 60 minutes to burn from one end to the other. But the burn rate is NOT constant along the rope, and the two ropes are not identical to each other. So you cannot burn half a rope and call it half an hour — in the video the flame reaches the halfway point after 40 minutes, not 30. Length tells you nothing about time.

You have a lighter. Nothing else. No ruler, no scissors, no watch.

Measure exactly 45 minutes.
