Three jars, every label wrong.

One jar holds apples, one holds oranges, one holds a mix of both. The labels have all been swapped, and not a single one is on the right jar.

You can reach into any jar and take out a fruit, without seeing inside. Then you have to label all three correctly.

Most people start planning several draws and a decision tree. You need far fewer than that - and there is exactly one jar it makes sense to reach into first.

The video stops before the number. How many fruits must you see, and which jar do you reach into?
