Higher of two dice

puzzlesposted 20 Aug 2026

Roll two dice, keep the higher. Or keep the lower. How far apart are the two average scores?

One way: roll two dice and keep the HIGHER number. The other: roll two dice and keep the LOWER number. A single ordinary die, rolled many times, averages three and a half.

So what does keep-the-higher average, what does keep-the-lower average, and how big is the gap? The two averages are cleaner than you would guess.
