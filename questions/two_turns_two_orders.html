Same two cube turns, reversed. Why do the endings split? Answer's in bio - free, takes an email.

Both cubes start with the same coloured, numbered faces showing. Then both get exactly two quarter-turns: one around the vertical axis, one around a horizontal axis.

The only difference is the order. One cube takes the vertical turn first. The other takes the horizontal turn first. By the end, the visible faces are plainly not the same.

The reel stops there because the important question is what that split says about combining turns in space.
