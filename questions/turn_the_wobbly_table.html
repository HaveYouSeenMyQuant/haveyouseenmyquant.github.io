Turn the wobbly table

A cafe table wobbles on bumpy ground. Can you always fix it just by TURNING it?

One foot never quite reaches. Three feet hold the table; the fourth hangs over a dip, so it tips one way, then the other. You could wedge a napkin under the short leg. But leave every foot where it is and just spin the whole table on the spot.

As it turns, the wobble changes. The question: can turning ALWAYS stop it, on any bumpy ground — and how far might you ever have to turn?

Most people say you'd get lucky sometimes. The real answer is a clean, surprising guarantee, and it turns on one idea you already trust.
