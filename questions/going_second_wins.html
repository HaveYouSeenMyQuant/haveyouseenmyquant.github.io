Going second wins

game_theoryposted 5 Aug 2026

Pick any run of three coin flips. I pick second and I beat you every time.

We flip a fair coin until one of the two runs appears. Whoever's shows up first wins. Sounds fifty-fifty. It is not:

HHH → THH I win 7/8 HHT → THH I win 3/4 HTH → HHT I win 2/3 HTT → HHT I win 2/3 THH → TTH I win 2/3 THT → TTH I win 2/3 TTH → HTT I win 3/4 TTT → HTT I win 7/8

My worst case across all eight of your options is still 2 wins in 3. Against HHH it is 7 in 8.

Each run has probability 1/8 of appearing in three given flips - that part is true, and it is also the trap. It does not settle a RACE, because the runs overlap themselves differently.
