Cubes add to a square

number_theoryposted 14 Aug 2026

Add up the first 100 cubes and you get exactly 5,050 squared.

1 = 1² 1 + 8 = 9 = 3² 1 + 8 + 27 = 36 = 6² 1 + 8 + 27 + 64 = 100 = 10²

It never misses. A pile of CUBES always adds up to a perfect SQUARE.

And look at the roots: 1, 3, 6, 10 - those are the running totals of 1, 2, 3, 4. So the first 100 cubes come to 25,502,500, and 5,050 is just 1+2+...+100.

There is a one-glance picture that makes this obvious, and it is much better than the algebra: induction can confirm a formula you already guessed, but it never tells you where the formula came from.
