A diagnostic threshold has no single best place

statisticsposted 3 Aug 2026

A test catches more sick people only by scaring more healthy ones. Solve the curve in bio - free, takes an email.

The setup is a diagnostic score. Red dots are sick people. Blue dots are healthy people. Higher scores look more suspicious, but the two clouds overlap.

Pick one vertical cutoff. Everything to the right is called positive. Move the cutoff left and you catch more of the sick cloud. The catch is that you also sweep up more healthy people, so false alarms rise at the same time.

Move the cutoff right and both numbers fall. That is why there is no threshold that is simply best without saying what a miss costs and what a false alarm costs.

The lower curve is the whole trade: false alarms across the bottom, sick people caught up the side. The video stops at the question: what does the area under that curve actually measure?
