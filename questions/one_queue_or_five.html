One queue or five

everydayposted 7 Aug 2026

One queue for five tills beats five queues by seven times.

Same tills. Same customers. Same service speed. The only thing that changes is whether people form five lines or one.

Average wait, five separate queues: 4.00 service times. One queue feeding five tills: 0.55.

And it gets better with more tills — 2.2x at two, 7.2x at five, 14x at eight.

The single queue looks longer, which is why it feels worse. It is longer. It also moves five times faster.
