1,000 bottles, one poisoned, ten tasters, one round

puzzlesposted 30 Jul 2026

1,000 bottles of wine. Exactly one is poisoned. You get 10 tasters and ONE round of testing. Find the bad bottle.

The rules, exactly:

• There are 1,000 bottles and precisely one of them is spiked. They look and taste identical. • You have 10 tasters. Each taster can sip from as many bottles as you like — one, a hundred, all of them. • A single sip of the bad bottle is enough to make someone react. Nothing else does. • The reaction only shows up hours later. So everyone drinks once, you wait, and you read the result once.

That last rule is the whole puzzle. You cannot test, wait, test again, and narrow it down — you get one round. Ten people, one reading, a thousand suspects.

Most people say it is impossible. It isn't, and ten is exactly the number you need — nine genuinely cannot do it.
