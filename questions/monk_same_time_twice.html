Same spot, same time

puzzlesposted 5 Aug 2026

A monk climbs on Monday and comes down on Tuesday.

He leaves the bottom at 6am on Monday and reaches the top by 6pm. He rests when he feels like it, walks fast or slowly, and can even turn back downhill for a while.

On Tuesday he leaves the TOP at 6am, comes down the very same path, and is at the bottom by 6pm. Again at any pace he likes.

Question: does there have to be a time of day at which he stood at exactly the same height on both days?

Most people say it depends on how he walked, or that you could arrange the two days to avoid it. Try to arrange it and you will find something strange happening.

There is a way of looking at this that settles it in one sentence, with no calculation at all - and it never tells you WHEN the moment is.
