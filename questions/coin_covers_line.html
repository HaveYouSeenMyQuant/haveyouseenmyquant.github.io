A coin exactly as wide as the floor gap

puzzlesposted 3 Aug 2026

A coin as wide as the floor gap looks like it should miss.

Setup: the floor has parallel lines, equally spaced. The coin's diameter is exactly that spacing. Drop the coin at a random place.

The useful move is to ignore left and right, because the lines run forever. Fold the centre of the coin into a single gap between two neighbouring lines.

Now the whole question is just about where that centre can be inside the gap, and how far the rim reaches from there. The video stops before the final share.
