Fold paper to the Moon.

A sheet of paper is a tenth of a millimetre thick. Fold it in half and it doubles. Fold it again and it doubles again.

Seven folds is about a finger. Seventeen is taller than a house. Around twenty-seven you have passed Everest, and a couple more puts you past the edge of space.

It is still the same sheet of paper the whole time - nothing has been added, it has only been halved.

The video stops before the reveal: how many folds does it take to reach the Moon, 384,400 km away? Almost everyone guesses in the hundreds or thousands. The real number is small enough to shock.
