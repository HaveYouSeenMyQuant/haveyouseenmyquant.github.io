The worst shot goes first

puzzlesposted 4 Aug 2026

Three shooters, and you are the worst of them.

You hit one shot in three. The second hits two in three. The third never misses.

You shoot first - precisely because you are the worst - then the second, then the third, round and round. Anyone still standing may shoot at whoever they like.

It is your turn. What do you do?

Nearly everyone takes aim at the deadliest one. It is defensible, and the arithmetic says it is not the best move you have. There is an option most people do not even count as a move.
