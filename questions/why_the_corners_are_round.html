Why the corners are round

everydayposted 7 Aug 2026

A hole's size does not matter. Its corners do.

Pull a metal sheet and cut a round hole in it. The metal beside the hole feels exactly 3x the stress — and that is true for a pinhole and for a hole a metre across. Making the hole smaller does not help at all.

Give the same hole square corners and it jumps to about 12x. Same metal, same pull, same opening.

It is why every window on every pressurised aircraft has rounded corners. Look next time.
