The leaves go the wrong way

physicsposted 8 Aug 2026

Stir a cup of tea and the leaves gather in the middle, not at the edge.

Everything about it says they should go outward. The tea is spinning. A soaked tea leaf is heavier than the tea around it. Spinning things throw heavy things to the rim — that is exactly how a spin dryer gets your clothes dry.

They go to the middle anyway, every time, and settle into a neat little heap you could cover with a penny.

Watch the surface of the tea while it spins: it really does climb the wall of the cup and sink in the middle. So the tea is being thrown outward in the same picture in which the leaves crawl inward, and that is the whole puzzle.
