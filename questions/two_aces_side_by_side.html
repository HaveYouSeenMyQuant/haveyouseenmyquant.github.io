Two aces side by side

puzzlesposted 18 Aug 2026

Shuffle the four aces into a deck. Chance at least two land side by side?

Pull the four aces, shuffle them back in, and spread the whole deck out face down. The aces are hidden in the row — usually with other cards between them.

So how likely is it that at least two aces ended up right next to each other? Most people guess it's rare. It happens far more often than that — a classic bar bet.
