Every step of the reasoning is correct. The answer loses.

Everyone picks a number from 0 to 100. Whoever lands closest to two-thirds of the average wins.

Random guesses average 50, so play 33. But everyone works that out, so play 22. Then 15, then 10, then 7 — and the chain does not stop until it reaches 0.

0 is the unique equilibrium. Play it against real people and you will not come close.
