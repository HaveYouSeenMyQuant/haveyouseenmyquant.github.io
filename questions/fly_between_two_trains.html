The fly between two trains

puzzlesposted 4 Aug 2026

Two trains, a fly, and one line of arithmetic.

Two trains are 100 km apart on the same track, each doing 50 km/h straight at the other.

A fly leaves the front of one train at 75 km/h, reaches the other train, turns round instantly, flies back, turns again - on and on, in ever shorter hops, until the trains meet.

How far has the fly flown?

You can set up the infinite series. It works, and it is the long way round. There is a way to see it that takes one line and no series at all, and the whole point of the puzzle is noticing which quantity is already pinned down.
