The ant on the stretching rope

puzzlesposted 4 Aug 2026

An ant crawls 1cm a second. Its rope grows 1m a second.

A rubber rope, 1 metre long, ant at the near end.

Every second: the ant crawls 1 cm, then the whole rope is stretched by another metre - and the stretch carries the ant along with it.

After a minute the rope is 61 metres and the ant has managed 60 centimetres of crawling. The gap ahead of it is bigger every single second, forever.

So does it ever reach the far end?

Nearly everyone answers instantly, and nearly everyone is answering a different question from the one that was asked. There is one change of viewpoint that settles it in a line.
