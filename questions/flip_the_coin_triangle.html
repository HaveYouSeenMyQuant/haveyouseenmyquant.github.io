Flip the coin triangle

puzzlesposted 20 Aug 2026

Ten coins make a triangle pointing up. Flip it to point down in the fewest moves?

The coins sit one at the top, then rows of two, then more below, down to a base of four. You want the whole triangle to point DOWN, by sliding coins to new spots.

Each move slides one coin, and most people's first guess is too many. So what is the true minimum -- and which coins do you move? The answer is smaller than it looks.
