A job that is 95% parallel, on a thousand cores — how much faster?

cs_systemsposted 1 Aug 2026

95% of this job splits across cores. Throw a thousand cores at it and it does NOT get a thousand times faster.

The setup, in full: a job takes one hour. Ninety-five percent of that work can be chopped up and run on as many cores as you like, all at the same time. The last five percent cannot — it has to happen in order, start to finish, on one core.

Two cores runs it nearly twice as fast. Four is faster again. Eight faster again. The video draws all four of those honestly, at true length.

So you buy a thousand cores. How much faster does the job finish?

Almost everyone guesses far too high, and the answer is not close. It also has a hard ceiling that no amount of hardware gets past — which is the real lesson, and the reason "just add more machines" quietly stops working.
