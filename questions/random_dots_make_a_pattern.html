Random dots make a pattern

geometryposted 14 Aug 2026

Random dots, one simple rule, and the same picture appears every single time.

Mark three corners. Put a dot anywhere. Then repeat forever: pick one of the three corners AT RANDOM, and move your dot halfway towards it.

Every step is an honest coin toss with three sides. Knowing the first thousand dots tells you nothing about the next one.

And yet after a few thousand dots the same figure is on the screen, whatever you do and wherever you started.

Checked rather than eyeballed: 60,000 generated points, every one addressed down to 6,561 sub-triangles, and not a single one lands where the figure has a hole.
