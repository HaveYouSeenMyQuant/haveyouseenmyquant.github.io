Eight people, over the limit

statisticsposted 8 Aug 2026

A lift rated for 8 people is over its limit about half the time.

630 kg for 8 persons works out at 78.75 kg each. The average adult weighs about 78. So the number on the plate is pitched almost exactly at what eight ordinary adults actually weigh — and half of all loads land above it.

A bigger lift does not fix it. Rate 20 people the same way and it is still a coin flip, because the limit and the load grow together.
