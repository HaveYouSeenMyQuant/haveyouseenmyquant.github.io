Two pointers trap rain water without looking ahead

cs_systemsposted 5 Aug 2026

Twelve walls trap rain, but the code never looks ahead.

The row is the classic water-trapping interview problem, but the trick is not to scan every column twice.

Keep a pointer at each end. Keep the tallest wall you have already seen from the left, and the tallest wall you have already seen from the right.

Each step compares the two current walls. If the left wall is shorter, the unknown stuff on the far right cannot be what limits this column. The left side decides how much water can sit here, so the code fills that column and moves on. If the right wall is shorter, the same argument works from the other end.
