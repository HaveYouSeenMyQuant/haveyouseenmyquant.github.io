The snail in the well

puzzlesposted 5 Aug 2026

A snail climbs 3ft a day and slips 2ft a night. 30ft well.

It starts at the bottom. Every day it climbs three feet. Every night, while it sleeps, it slides back two.

How many days before it is out?

The gain is one foot a day and the well is thirty feet deep, so almost everyone answers thirty. It is not thirty, and the reason has nothing to do with the arithmetic being hard.

Something happens on the final day that does not happen on any of the others, and the moment you see what it is, the whole thing takes about five seconds.

Worth knowing beyond the puzzle: it is the mistake people make when they average a process that STOPS. The average describes almost every step and is completely wrong about the last one.
