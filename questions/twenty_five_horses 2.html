Twenty-five horses, five tracks

puzzlesposted 6 Aug 2026

25 horses, 5 tracks, no stopwatch. Fewest races to find the top 3?

You can race five horses at a time. There is no clock, so a race tells you the ORDER those five finished in and nothing else - no times, nothing you can compare between races. Every horse is consistent: the same horse always beats the same horses.

Find the fastest three, in order, in as few races as you can.

Nearly everyone lands on the same plan - race the five groups, then race the winners, take that podium. It is one race short of right, and the podium it gives you can be wrong.

The horse it misses is the one that lost a single race, to the eventual champion, and was never seen again.

The trick is not ranking the horses. It is proving which ones cannot possibly be on the podium, which is far cheaper.
