Ten stacks, one weighing

puzzlesposted 5 Aug 2026

10 stacks of coins, one all fakes, one weighing.

A real coin weighs 10 g. A fake weighs 9 g. One whole stack is fake, the other nine are genuine.

The scale reads a number - it is not a balance. You switch it on once, and you get one reading.

Weighing one coin from each stack in turn is ten goes. Splitting the stacks in half and halving again is three or four. One does not look possible at all.

It is, and the trick is not about weighing. It is about deciding what the number will MEAN before you take the reading.
