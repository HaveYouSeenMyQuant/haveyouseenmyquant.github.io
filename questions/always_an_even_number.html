Always an even number

graphsposted 6 Aug 2026

However people shake hands, the number who shook an odd number of times is always even.

Let a room full of people shake hands however they like. Count how many hands each person shook.

Some of those counts are odd. Now count how many people have an odd count — it is never 1, never 3, never 5.

Checked exhaustively: every possible set of handshakes among 2 to 6 people, all 33,866 of them, and not one exception.

And it is specifically about ODD — the number of people with an EVEN count is frequently odd, so this is not a fact about tallies in general.
