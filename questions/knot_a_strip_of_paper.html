Tie a knot in a strip of paper, flatten it -- what neat shape do you get?

Take a long paper ribbon, tie one ordinary overhand knot in it, pull it snug and press it flat on the table.

The flattened knot makes a crisp, perfectly regular shape -- exactly, not roughly. Which shape is it? You can check the answer with a till receipt in about ten seconds.
