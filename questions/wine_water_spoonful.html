A spoonful each way

puzzlesposted 4 Aug 2026

A spoon of wine into the water, a spoon back.

Two glasses, the same amount in each. One is wine, one is water.

Take a spoonful of the wine and stir it into the water. Now take a spoonful of that mixture and stir it back into the wine.

Both glasses hold what they started with. But there is wine in the water now, and water in the wine. Which glass is more polluted?

Most people reach for the algebra. It works, and it is the wrong way in - there is a one-line argument that never mentions the spoon, never mentions stirring, and settles it instantly.
