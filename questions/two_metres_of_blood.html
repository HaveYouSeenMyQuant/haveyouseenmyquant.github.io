Two metres of blood

biologyposted 8 Aug 2026

A giraffe's head is 2 m above its heart.

A heart is a pump. Blood is a liquid, and liquids are heavy — so every single beat, a giraffe's pump has to hold up a two-metre tube of blood before its brain gets a drop.

Your own heart has about thirty centimetres to manage. Same job, same liquid, very different height.

Work out what the height costs and you can predict what a giraffe's blood must be doing — then check it against what has actually been measured in a real one.
