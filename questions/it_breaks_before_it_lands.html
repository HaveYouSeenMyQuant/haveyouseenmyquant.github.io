It breaks before it lands

everydayposted 7 Aug 2026

A falling chimney breaks in mid-air, before it hits anything.

Watch any tall brick chimney being brought down. It does not come over in one piece — it snaps while it is still in the air, often when it is only thirty or forty degrees past vertical.

And it breaks about a third of the way up, almost every time.

It is not impact damage and it is not a second charge. The chimney does it to itself on the way down.
