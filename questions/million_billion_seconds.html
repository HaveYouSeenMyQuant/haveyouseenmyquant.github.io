A million seconds against a billion

everydayposted 6 Aug 2026

A million seconds is 11 days. A billion seconds is 31 years.

Same arithmetic for both: 86,400 seconds in a day, 365.2425 days in a year. A million seconds is 11.6 days - about a week and a half ago. A billion seconds is 31.7 years.

A trillion, while we are here, is 31,688 years. Further back than farming.

The numbers are not the interesting part. This is: you already knew a billion was a thousand millions, and the comparison still landed. Knowing the definition does not help.

There is a reason those two words slip past each other, it is a real feature of how people process number rather than a gap in anyone's education, and there is one reliable defence against it.

It matters more than trivia: a £3 million budget and a £3 billion one get discussed in the same tone of voice. So do a one-in-a-million risk and a one-in-a-billion one.
