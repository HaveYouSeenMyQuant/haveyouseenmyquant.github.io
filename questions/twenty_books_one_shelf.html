Twenty books, more orders than seconds since the Big Bang

everydayposted 6 Aug 2026

20 books on a shelf have more orders than there have been seconds since the Big Bang.

20 choices for the first slot, 19 for the next, 18 for the one after. That gives 2,432,902,008,176,640,000 different shelves.

The universe is 13.797 billion years old, which is 435,391,266,744,000,000 seconds. The shelf wins by about five and a half times.

Nineteen books would not have done it - nineteen comes to about a third of the universe's age. One extra book was worth a factor of twenty.

That growth is the actual subject, and it is the wall every brute-force search hits: trying all orders of twenty things is not slow, it is impossible, and no faster computer changes that.
