52! — the number of ways to shuffle a deck

combinatoricsposted 28 Jul 2026

Shuffle a deck of cards. That exact order has almost certainly never existed before in history 🃏

Because there are 52! ways to do it:

80,658,175,170,943,878,571,660,636,856,403,766,975,289,505,440,883,277,824,000,000,000,000

A 68-digit number. If all 8 billion of us had shuffled a deck every second since the Big Bang, we'd still have covered less than 10⁻⁴⁰ of them.

Can you crack it?
