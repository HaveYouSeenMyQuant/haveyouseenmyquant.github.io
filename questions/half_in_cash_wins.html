Half in cash wins

financeposted 5 Aug 2026

A stock that goes nowhere, plus cash that earns nothing, compounds at 6% a year.

The stock doubles or halves each year, 50/50. Its average return looks like +25%. What it actually compounds at is ZERO - over fifty years the typical path ends exactly where it started.

Now hold half of it and half cash, rebalancing to an even split every year. Same stock, same cash, and the portfolio ends up 19x.

Neither ingredient grows. The mixture does.

Before anyone tries this: it needs violent volatility (a 2x/0.5x asset is far wilder than any index), it needs rebalancing to be nearly free, and it LOSES against a stock that actually grows. The useful part is not the strategy - it is which average you plan with.
