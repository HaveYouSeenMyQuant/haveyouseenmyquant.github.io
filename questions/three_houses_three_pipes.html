Three houses, three pipes

puzzlesposted 8 Aug 2026

Connect 3 houses to 3 utilities with no crossings. You will always get 8 of the 9.

Every house needs gas, water and electricity. No two lines may cross. Nine connections in total.

Eight is easy once you see it. The ninth never fits - and not because you are not clever enough with the routing. It cannot be done at all, by anyone, ever.

That is a much stronger claim than 'this is hard', and it needs a different kind of argument: you cannot prove impossibility by failing to find a solution.

The graph has a name, and it turns out to be one of only TWO patterns that make any drawing impossible to flatten.
