A trillion ancestors that never existed

everydayposted 6 Aug 2026

40 generations back your family tree wants a trillion people. There weren't.

Two parents. Four grandparents. Eight great-grandparents. The count doubles every step back, and doubling does not stay small.

Forty generations is roughly a thousand years. The arithmetic says 1,099,511,627,776 ancestors - all alive at the same moment.

About 300 million people were alive in the year 1000. Every human who has EVER lived comes to around 117 billion. Your family tree is asking for nine times that, standing in one place, in one century.

The doubling is not wrong. Every one of those steps is right. Something else in the setup is false, and it is something so obvious nobody states it out loud - which is exactly why it slips past.

Worth knowing beyond the trivia: it is the same mistake as multiplying counts that are not independent, and it hides in risk models the same way it hides in family trees.
