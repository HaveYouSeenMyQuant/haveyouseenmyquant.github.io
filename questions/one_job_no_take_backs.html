One job, no take-backs

puzzlesposted 18 Aug 2026

One job, applicants one at a time, no take-backs. When do you hire?

Each person you interview, you must decide on the spot: hire them and stop, or reject them for good. You can't call back anyone you turned down, and you can't see who's still to come.

You want the single best applicant of the whole line. So how many should you interview before you commit and hire the best you've seen?

Stop too early and you don't know what good looks like yet; stop too late and the best has probably already walked out. There's a sweet spot — and a strategy that beats it surprisingly often.
