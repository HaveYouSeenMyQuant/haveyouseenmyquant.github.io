How far does the ball bounce?

puzzlesposted 18 Aug 2026

A ball dropped from 1 m bounces 3/4 as high each time, forever. Total distance?

It hits the floor, springs back to three-quarters of its height, falls, bounces again to three-quarters of that, and keeps going — smaller and smaller, without ever quite stopping.

Add up every rise and every fall. How far does the ball travel in total?

It bounces infinitely many times, so it feels like the distance should be endless. It isn't — it's a plain, finite number, and a surprisingly tidy one.
