The step size that breaks gradient descent.

Roll a ball down a bowl by taking steps downhill. Tiny steps work but crawl. Bigger steps get there faster, and there is one size that drops onto the bottom in a single jump.

Go bigger than that and it overshoots onto the opposite wall, then bounces back and forth, settling more and more slowly.

Keep going and there is a step size where the bounces stop shrinking at all - and past it they grow, and the whole thing diverges. Nothing about the bowl changed. Only the step size did.
