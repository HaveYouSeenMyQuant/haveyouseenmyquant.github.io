A hundred lockers, a hundred people — which end up open?

puzzlesposted 31 Jul 2026

A hundred lockers, all shut. A hundred people walk past. Which lockers end up open?

The rules, in full: the lockers are numbered 1 to 100 and every one starts closed. Person 1 walks the corridor and opens all hundred. Person 2 flips every 2nd locker — 2, 4, 6, 8 — and flipping means shut becomes open, open becomes shut. Person 3 flips every 3rd. Person 4 every 4th. All the way to person 100, who flips locker 100 and goes home.

After the last person, some lockers are open and some are shut. Which ones are open?

Almost nobody guesses the pattern, and it is not the one you would expect from all that chaos. There is a reason, and once you see it you cannot unsee it — no calculation needed, and it works for a corridor of any length.
