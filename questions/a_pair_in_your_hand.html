A pair in your hand

puzzlesposted 18 Aug 2026

Deal five cards. How likely is it that at least two share the same value?

A 'match' just means two cards of the same value — two nines, two kings — never mind the suit. Most hands look like five different values all jumbled together.

But how often do two of the five secretly agree? Most people guess it's rare. It is much more common than that — and the reason is a famous one.
