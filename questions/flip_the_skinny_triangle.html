The skinny triangle flip test

geometryposted 4 Aug 2026

This skinny triangle flips when one dot falls inside a circle. Answer's in bio - takes an email.

Four dots can be split into two triangles in exactly two ways. The bad split makes two long slivers.

The test is beautifully local. Pick one triangle and draw the circle through its three corners. If the fourth dot falls inside that circle, the diagonal you drew fails, so you flip to the other diagonal.

After the flip, the opposite dot is outside the new circle, and the slivers open up. On a larger scatter, you do the same test again and again until the mesh stops wanting to flip.
