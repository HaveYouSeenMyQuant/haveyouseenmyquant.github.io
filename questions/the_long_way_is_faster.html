The long way is faster

physicsposted 5 Aug 2026

Same start, same finish. The LONGER ramp wins by 35%.

One track is a straight line - the shortest route between the two points. The other bulges downward, dips BELOW the finish, and has to climb back up.

straight: 1.03 m in 0.931 s curved: 1.19 m in 0.606 s

35% faster over a path 15% longer.

The instinct that says shortest = fastest is quietly assuming constant speed. Once your speed depends on the path itself, the shortest route and the quickest route stop being the same thing - which is also why light bends when it changes medium.
