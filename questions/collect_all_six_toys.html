Collect all six toys

puzzlesposted 17 Aug 2026

Six toys to collect, one per cereal box, at random. How many boxes?

Every box hides one of six little toys, each equally likely, and you want the full set.

The first toys come quick. But near the end almost every box is a duplicate, and you sit there opening box after box waiting for that one last toy you're missing.

Most people guess a number not far off six. The real average is a lot bigger than that — the tail, where you're chasing the last toy or two, quietly eats most of your boxes.
