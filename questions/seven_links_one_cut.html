Seven links, seven days

puzzlesposted 4 Aug 2026

A 7-link gold chain, one link a day for 7 days.

The innkeeper will not be paid early and will not be paid late. At the end of day n he must be holding exactly n links.

He is happy to give change - hand him a bigger piece and he hands smaller ones back.

Opening a link frees that link and splits the chain either side of it. Cutting is expensive.

How few links do you have to cut?

Most people get to two and stop, because they are thinking about what they hand over. The question is only ever what he is HOLDING at the end of the day, and change works in both directions.

The video stops before the answer. How few, and which?
