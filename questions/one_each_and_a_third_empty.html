One each, and a third empty

probabilityposted 8 Aug 2026

One ball per bin on average — and a third of the bins get nothing.

A hundred balls thrown into a hundred bins, completely at random. The average is exactly one. About thirty-seven bins end up empty, and the busiest holds four.

Nobody aimed. Randomness alone produces a spread most people would read as evidence that something was rigged.
