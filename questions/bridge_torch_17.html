Four people, one torch, seventeen minutes

puzzlesposted 30 Jul 2026

Four people, one torch, a bridge that holds two. They cross in 1, 2, 5 and 10 minutes. Get all four over in 17.

Read the rules carefully, because almost everyone lands on 19.

It's pitch dark. The bridge holds at most two people at a time, and nobody can cross without the torch — so after a pair goes over, somebody has to walk it back.

A pair moves at the slower one's pace: send the 1 and the 10 together and that crossing costs 10 minutes, not 1.

Four people. 1, 2, 5 and 10 minutes. All of them across in 17.

The obvious plan — let the fastest walker escort everyone, one at a time — gets you 19, and 19 feels like it must be the answer. It isn't.
