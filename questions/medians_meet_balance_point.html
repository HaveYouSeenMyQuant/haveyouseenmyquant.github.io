Three triangle lines should miss. Solve where they meet in bio - it costs an email.

Take any triangle. From each corner, draw the line to the midpoint of the opposite side. Two of those lines cross somewhere ordinary. The surprise is that the third line does not make a tiny triangle of near-misses - it passes through the same point.

Now drag the corners around. Make the triangle tall, obtuse, or nearly flat. The same meeting point keeps moving, but it never splits into three crossings. It is also the balance point of the whole triangle.
