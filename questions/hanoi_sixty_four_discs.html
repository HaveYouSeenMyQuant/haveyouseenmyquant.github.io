Sixty-four discs

puzzlesposted 8 Aug 2026

64 discs, one move a second, and you finish in 585 billion years.

Three pegs. Move the whole stack to the far peg. One disc at a time, and never a bigger disc onto a smaller one.

4 discs: 15 moves. 10 discs: 1,023. Every disc you add DOUBLES it.

So 64 discs takes 18,446,744,073,709,551,615 moves. At one a second that is about 585 billion years - the Sun has roughly 5 billion years of hydrogen left, so this outlasts it 117 times over.

The interesting part is not the size. It is that this count is FORCED: no cleverer method exists, and the reason why is a nicer argument than the number.
