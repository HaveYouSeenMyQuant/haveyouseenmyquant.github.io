Still, and doing a thousand

everydayposted 6 Aug 2026

Sitting still in London you are doing 1,043 km/h.

At the equator it is 1,674. At either pole it is zero. Same planet, same rotation — and in London you are outrunning the airliner overhead without moving a muscle.

One factor explains all three numbers, and it is not the one most retellings reach for: using a 24-hour day instead of the real one changes the answer by 0.27%, while the factor that actually matters costs London 38%.
