Where do the balls pile up?

puzzlesposted 18 Aug 2026

Balls bounce randomly down a board of pegs. Where do they pile up?

Every ball hits peg after peg, and at each one it goes left or right at random, like a coin flip, all the way down into the slots below.

Drop thousands of them. Do they spread out evenly, bunch up in the middle, or heap at the two edges?

Each ball's path is pure chaos — yet the pile they make together is one of the most orderly, most famous shapes in all of maths, and the reason why is gorgeous.
