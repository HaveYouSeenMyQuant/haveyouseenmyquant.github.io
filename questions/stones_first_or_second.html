Twenty-one stones

puzzlesposted 5 Aug 2026

21 stones. Take 1, 2 or 3. Last stone wins.

Twenty-one stones sit on the table. You and I take turns, and on your turn you remove one, two or three of them - your choice, every time.

Whoever takes the last stone wins.

Now the only question: would you rather go first, or second?

Most people treat this as a toss-up, or assume it needs a long analysis of who does what. It is neither. One of those two seats wins against ANY play by the other - not usually, not with luck, always - and whoever sits in it barely has to think after the opening move.

The way in is to stop looking at the start and look at the END: which piles would you hate to be handed?
