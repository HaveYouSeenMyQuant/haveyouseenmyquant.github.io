1,000 days from a Monday — what day is it?

number_theoryposted 30 Jul 2026

Start on a Monday. What day is it 1,000 days later? No calendar, no calculator — you can do this one in your head.

A week is a wheel of seven. Spin it enough times and you land back exactly where you started, so almost all of those 1,000 days cancel out completely. Only what's left over at the end moves the day at all.
