A die pays you the number times itself. Is £13 a go a good price?

A seaside stall rolls one die and pays you in pounds: roll 2 and it pays 4, roll 3 and it pays 9, roll 6 and it pays 36. Roll a 1 and you get 1.

Every face is just as likely. One go costs £13, handed over before you roll. Good bet or bad bet? Most people get this one backwards.
