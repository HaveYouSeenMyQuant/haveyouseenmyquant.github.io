The balloon leans the wrong way

everydayposted 7 Aug 2026

A car brakes hard. Which way does a helium balloon go?

You lurch forward. Your coffee slides forward. Every loose thing in the car goes forward.

The balloon does not — and the reason is one vector that explains every object in the car at once, with the objects only deciding which end of it they go to.

Worked properly rather than argued in words: the first draft of this had the direction the wrong way round, and only writing the effective gravity down caught it.

Hang a pendulum next to the balloon and they tilt by the same angle in opposite directions — 26.6° at a half-g stop.
