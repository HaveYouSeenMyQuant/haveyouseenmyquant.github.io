The drunkard on the cliff

puzzlesposted 17 Aug 2026

A drunk man on a clifftop, one step from the edge. Does he fall?

He is walking home in the dark, along a path on top of a sea cliff. He starts one step from the edge. Every few seconds he sways and takes a step, and which way is a pure coin toss: toward the edge, or back toward safety.

He never sobers up and never gets drunker. Just one random step after another, all night long.

Here is the trap. His steps are fair, so on average he drifts nowhere at all. Your gut says he is probably fine, weaving back and forth somewhere in the middle. Is your gut right — or does something surprising happen once the steps never stop?
