A Gaussian-process band pinches wherever data lands

ml_fundamentalsposted 3 Aug 2026

A measured point makes uncertainty pinch. Solve the far-away band in bio - answer costs an email.

The setup is a Gaussian-process model. That means the prediction is not a single line. It is a whole family of smooth curves, shown as a moving shaded band.

When one measurement lands, every curve that survives has to pass through that point. The uncertainty vanishes at the dot, and nearby locations get pulled tight because nearby values are linked.

When a second measurement lands, the same rule applies again. The band pinches at both dots, while the sample curves keep moving inside the space that is still allowed.

The clip stops at the far-away question: when you are infinitely far from every measured point, what should the band do?
