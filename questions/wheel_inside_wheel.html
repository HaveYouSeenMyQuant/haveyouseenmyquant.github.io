Mark one point on the small rim and roll it round the inside without slipping. Every single thing in the picture is curved: the small wheel, the track it runs on, the spoke swinging round.

So the marked point should trace something curved. A loop, a petal, a flower - that is what almost everyone says.

It does not. And the reason is that the small wheel's own spin exactly cancels its trip around the inside, which only happens at one particular ratio of the two sizes.
