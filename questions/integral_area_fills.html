The area is the number the rectangles head toward

calculusposted 5 Aug 2026

Rectangles chase a curved area but never catch it.

The setup is a curve above an axis. The area underneath is one number, but a first row of rectangles is only a rough guess. Their flat tops stick through the curve in some places and leave gaps in others.

Halve the widths and every old rectangle becomes two thinner ones. The jagged roof hugs the curve more closely. Halve again and the change is smaller. Keep going and the rectangle total heads toward one fixed value.

That is the point of an integral: not a magic formula, but the number these rectangle totals settle toward. The video stops before the number.
