The man who solved the market

historyposted 18 Aug 2026

A geometer and codebreaker bet the market hid a code.

He left mathematics and built the most successful fund ever — hiring astronomers and physicists instead of traders, and letting machines hunt for faint patterns in oceans of data, thousands of tiny edges, repeated millions of times.

For about thirty years his fund earned so much, so steadily, that rival bankers didn't believe the numbers. Then he gave most of the fortune back to mathematics and science.

Who was he, what was the company — and just how high were those returns? The full story and the figure are on the site.
