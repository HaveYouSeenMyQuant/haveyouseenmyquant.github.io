Four litres from three and five

puzzlesposted 4 Aug 2026

A 3-litre jug and a 5-litre jug. Measure exactly 4.

Neither jug has any markings.

You may fill one to the brim, empty one out completely, or pour one into the other until the first runs out or the second is full. That is the whole list. No eyeballing half, no tilting.

There is no 4 in a 3 and no 4 in a 5, which is exactly why it bites.

The good version of this question is not "can you find four" - it is "which amounts are reachable at all", and that is settled before you pour anything.
