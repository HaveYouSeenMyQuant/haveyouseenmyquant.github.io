The hob will not help

physicsposted 9 Aug 2026

Up a mountain the water stops at 93C, and turning the hob up does nothing.

Put a pan of water on a camping stove at 2000 m and it starts bubbling at 93.4C instead of 100. That is the hottest your food will ever get up there, so the egg takes about 1.6 times as long. At 3500 m it takes over twice as long.

And the one thing that cannot rescue it is more heat. Turn the flame right up and you get a harder boil, faster steam, an emptier pan — and the same number on the thermometer.
