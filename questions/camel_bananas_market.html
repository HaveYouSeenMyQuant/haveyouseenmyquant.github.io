A camel, 3000 bananas and a market

puzzlesposted 4 Aug 2026

3000 bananas, one camel, 1000 miles.

The camel carries at most 1000 bananas at a time, and eats one banana for every mile it walks - in either direction. Bananas can be put down anywhere and picked up later.

The obvious answer is zero: 1000 bananas over 1000 miles is eaten exactly. The obvious answer is wrong, and so is almost everyone's second guess.

The whole thing turns on one idea: the cost of a mile is not one banana, it is one banana per TRIP - and how many trips you must make depends on how much is still behind you.

The video stops before the number. How many bananas reach the market?
