A hundred floors, two eggs: find the floor where they break

puzzlesposted 30 Jul 2026

A 100-floor tower. Two eggs. Somewhere there's a floor where an egg starts to break. Find it — how few drops?

Read the rules carefully, because the constraint is what makes this hard.

The eggs are identical. There is one floor F: dropped from F or anything above it, an egg breaks. Dropped from below F, it survives undamaged and you can drop it again. F could be any floor from 1 to 100 and you have to name it EXACTLY.

You have two eggs. A broken egg is gone — you don't get it back.

And your answer has to be a number of drops that works for every possible F. Not a lucky guess. Guaranteed.

One floor at a time from the bottom: up to 99 drops. Halving: the first egg breaks at 50 and now you have one egg and 49 floors to walk, so that costs 50. Even probing every 10th floor still costs 19.

You can do much better than all of them.
