Eight queens, none attacking

puzzlesposted 7 Aug 2026

8 queens on a chessboard. How many ways leave none attacking?

A queen takes anything on its row, its column, or either diagonal, all the way across the board.

There are 4,426,165,368 ways to drop eight queens onto sixty-four squares. How many of those leave every queen safe?

Both guesses people make are wrong, and they are wrong in opposite directions. Four billion sounds like it must leave plenty. The constraint sounds tight enough to leave almost nothing.

The real answer is small enough to write out on one page, and large enough that nobody has ever found them all by hand.

It is an interview fixture for a reason that has nothing to do with chess: checking all four billion is hopeless, and the fix is not a faster computer. It is refusing to build the arrangements that cannot work.
