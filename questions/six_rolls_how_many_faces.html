Six rolls, how many faces

puzzlesposted 21 Aug 2026

Roll one die six times. On average, how many different faces actually show up?

You roll a single die six times and write down each result. Because numbers repeat, you usually will not see all six faces -- some come up twice or more, others never appear.

So on average, how many different faces do you see across the six rolls? It is fewer than you would guess.
