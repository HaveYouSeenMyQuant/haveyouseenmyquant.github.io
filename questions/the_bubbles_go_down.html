The bubbles go down

physicsposted 7 Aug 2026

In a settling pint of stout the bubbles go DOWNWARDS.

Not an illusion, not the glass. Watch the ones against the wall and they are travelling down — while being lighter than the drink they are in. Do the same with a lager and it never happens.

Ten times the width is a hundred times the speed.
