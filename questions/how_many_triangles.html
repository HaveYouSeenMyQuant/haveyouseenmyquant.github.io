How many triangles

puzzlesposted 8 Aug 2026

A triangle cut into 16. How many triangles are in the picture?

Every side divided into four, which makes sixteen little triangles. But the little ones are not the answer.

Two of them together make a bigger triangle. Four make a bigger one again. The whole figure is one. And some of the triangles in there are pointing the other way up - those are the ones almost everybody misses.

Count every triangle, every size, both orientations.

Most people land on sixteen, or get part way into the bigger ones and lose track of which they have already had. Losing track is the actual difficulty - it is a bookkeeping problem wearing a geometry costume.

There is a way to organise the count so that you cannot double-count and cannot miss one, and it turns the whole thing into about ten seconds of arithmetic.
