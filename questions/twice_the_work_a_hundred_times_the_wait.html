Twice the work. A hundred times the wait.

A machine busy half the time keeps you waiting about one job. Give it twice as much work — busy ninety-nine per cent of the time — and the wait is ninety-nine jobs.

Not twice. Ninety-nine times. And the step from ninety to ninety-five per cent, where you still have spare capacity on paper, doubles it on its own.
