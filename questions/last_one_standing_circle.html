Forty-one in a circle

puzzlesposted 4 Aug 2026

41 people in a circle, every 2nd one out.

The counting starts at the top and goes round: one stays, the next steps out. When you come back round, the count carries straight on over the gaps - it never restarts.

Keep going and exactly one person is left standing. Where should you have stood?

There is a way to work it out without simulating anything, and it is one of those results that looks like a magic trick: write the number of people in binary and do one small thing to it.

The video stops before the position. Which place survives, and what is the trick?
