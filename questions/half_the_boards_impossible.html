Half the boards are impossible

puzzlesposted 7 Aug 2026

Swap two tiles on a finished sliding puzzle and it can never be solved again.

Not 'very hard'. Not 'needs a clever sequence'. Impossible, by anyone, forever.

From a solved 3x3 board you can reach exactly 181,440 of the 362,880 possible arrangements. Half. The other half - including the one with two tiles swapped - is sealed off.

This is why you cannot settle it by sliding. Sliding can only ever tell you that you have not succeeded YET. Something else has to rule out all 181,440 failures at once.

In the 1880s a puzzle-seller offered a large prize for solving a board with two tiles swapped. It was safe money.
