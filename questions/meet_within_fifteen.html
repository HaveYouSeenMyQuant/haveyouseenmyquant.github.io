Two friends, a random hour, a 15-minute wait — do they ever meet? — comment MEET and I'll DM you the answer

The setup, in full: two friends agree to meet somewhere between one o'clock and two. Neither of them says when. Each turns up at a completely random moment inside that hour, and neither knows anything about the other's plans.

Whoever gets there first waits fifteen minutes. If the other has not shown up by then, they give up and go home.

What are the chances they meet at all?

The usual first instinct is that fifteen minutes out of sixty is a quarter, so it must be about one in four. It isn't. The second instinct is that it must be a coin flip. It isn't quite that either.
