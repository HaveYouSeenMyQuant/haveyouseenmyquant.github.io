Step on the scales for your heart

An app that reads your resting heart rate and breathing rate from your phone's microphone and motion sensor. Sixty seconds, phone on your chest, once a day.

No watch, no strap, no subscription to something you already own a sensor for.

What it is honestly

  • Your phone's accelerometer can feel your chest move. Its microphone can hear you breathe.
  • From those it estimates a resting heart rate and a breathing rate, and shows you how they drift over weeks.
  • It is a wellness tool for spotting your own trends. It is not a medical device, it does not diagnose anything, and it is not a substitute for a clinician.
